Privacy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Block Puzzle is a small, single-player game. It does not ask you to make an account or provide personal information.

What the game stores

The game saves a few preferences in your browser's local storage so they are there next time:

  • Your best score
  • Your chosen colors (background, blocks and accent)
  • Whether game sounds are on or off, and the volume
  • Your drag assist and animation settings

Your current board and score stay in memory while the page is open. They are not sent to a server or saved for later.

Cookies, analytics, and sound

The game itself does not set cookies and does not include analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or third-party assets. Optional game sounds are generated in your browser and are not streamed from another service.

Offline copy

If you visit while online, the site may save its game and privacy pages in your browser's service-worker cache for offline use. You can remove this copy and the saved preferences by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Website hosting

The hosted website runs on GitHub Pages. Like other hosting providers, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details when it serves a page. This game does not receive or use that information. See GitHub's privacy statement for details about its handling of hosting requests.

Questions

For questions about this page, contact the project through its GitHub repository. Please do not post private information in a public issue.
